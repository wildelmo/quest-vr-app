exact processing). Please keep this file with any redistribution.

## Sky

- **The Milky Way panorama** (`assets/sky/milkyway_4k.jpg`, `milkyway_2k.jpg`)
  ESO / Serge Brunier (ESO/S. Brunier), <https://www.eso.org/public/images/eso0932a/>
  License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  Changes: downscaled from the 6000×3000 TIFF, a very mild lift of the darkest tones (black point
  0 → 4/255) so JPEG does not crush them; otherwise unmodified. Obtained via the MIT-licensed
  packaging by Daniel Dyrda (<https://github.com/dyrdadev/milky-way-skybox-for-unity>).
- **Yale Bright Star Catalog, 5th revised edition** (`assets/sky/stars.bin`, `stars.json`)
  Hoffleit, D. & Warren, W. H. Jr. (1991), Astronomical Data Center / Harvard–Smithsonian CfA,
  <http://tdc-www.harvard.edu/catalogs/bsc5.html>. Public catalog data.
  JSON conversion by Bretton Wade, <https://github.com/brettonw/YaleBrightStarCatalog> (MIT License).

## Audio

All recordings were converted to OGG Vorbis, trimmed, peak-normalised and (for the beds) cut into
seamless loops. Sources:

- **Ben Burnes (Abstraction)** — <https://abstractionmusic.com>, sample packs released as
  [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (attribution appreciated, not required):
  - *2HTC Samples Vol 4* and *Vol 4 Addendum* (recorded with Mark Powers): `pad_bioluminescence.ogg`
    (“Bioluminescence – Watery Pad”), `pad_northern_swell.ogg` and `pad_northern_brilliant.ogg`
    (“Swimming in the Northern Lights – Swell Pads / Brilliant Lights”), `pad_seafoam_waves.ogg`
    (“Seafoam – Quiet Waves”).
  - *Retail Therapy Sample Pack*: `bowl_1..7.ogg` (Ceramic Bowl 1–7), `glass_1.ogg` (Glass Glasses 1),
    `ting_1..4.ogg` (Silverware Ting 1–4).
  - *Micro Pack – Organic Wooshes*: `whoosh_gentle.ogg` (Gentle Swish), `whoosh_twirl_1..2.ogg` (Twirl Smol 1–2).
  - *Novice Cello (Nov 2021)*: `pluck_cello_c2.ogg`, `pluck_cello_g2.ogg`, `pluck_cello_d3.ogg`,
    `pluck_cello_a3.ogg` (pizzicato plucks).
  - *Bottle Plops (Apr 2021)*: `plop_airy_1.ogg`.
- **“100 CC0 SFX”, “40 CC0 water/splash/slime SFX” and “30 CC0 SFX loops”** packs, CC0 1.0, obtained via
  the lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds collection
  (<https://github.com/lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds>): `bell_1..3.ogg`, `gong_1..2.ogg`,
  `glass_2.ogg`, `splash_soft_1..4.ogg`, `bubbles_loop.ogg`, `water_loop_1.ogg`.
  Author: unknown — the collection ships these packs without a readme. Their names match the CC0 packs
  published by “rubberduck” on OpenGameArt, but this could not be verified while building, so they are
  credited as *unknown author, CC0 via lavenderdotpet/CC0-Public-Domain-Sounds*.
- **Wind** — felix.blume, freesound #139337, <https://freesound.org/people/felix.blume/sounds/139337/>,
  CC0 1.0 (`wind_loop.ogg`).
- **Stream** — mystiscool, freesound #7138, <https://freesound.org/people/mystiscool/sounds/7138/>,
  [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (`stream_loop.ogg`; edited into a 45 s loop).
  Both via Muges/ambientsounds (<https://github.com/Muges/ambientsounds>).
- Everything else you hear (crickets, water lapping, chimes, generative pads) is synthesised in Web Audio.
